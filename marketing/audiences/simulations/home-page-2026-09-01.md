## Splits (findings, not noise)

- **Stylization:** QM docks credibility for drawn screens while four others credit the *drawn* Part 11 modal as the page's most credible moment. Specific, standards-literate detail carries trust, drawn or not; the ask is one real recording where the conversion rule demands it, not de-stylizing the page.
- **Section 03:** two operators stop at the thesis opener; COO simultaneously calls the journey frame "genuinely the best section for me." Content works; the lead-in loses operators.
- **Harmonic Bionics film:** best asset (EM) and hostile witness (CIO — "engineering system of record" contradicts the coexistence story), same 83 seconds.
- **Attention cliffs by altitude:** operators stall at sec 03's thesis, evaluators at sec 05's directory; the film ledger recaptures four of six. Nobody reads the whole page closely.
- **Forwarding chain blocked both ways:** COO (nearest to economic buyer) shares *down* to quality; the gatekeepers would only forward *up* once layer-vs-suite and validation are answered.

## Surprises

1. The centerpiece visual idiom (governed conversation) is the page's second-largest objection factory (5/6).
2. Maxed recognition does not convert — QM at Recognition 9 still lands at 2; the bottleneck has moved past "know my world."
3. Zero demo intent, six deep clicks — and the two most trust-critical destinations ("audit-ready", "For IT") are off-page and untested by this run.
4. "AI-powered platform" exists only in the browser tab title; the body never makes the AI claim (CIO flagged; the reader the title targets).
5. The most-praised and most-litigated line is the same line ("your systems of record stay authoritative") — because section 04 argues with it.
