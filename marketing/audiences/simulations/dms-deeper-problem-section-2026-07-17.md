**Stimulus:** the reworked section 02 exploration at `/explorations/products/dms/deeper-problem`: header "Four symptoms, one root cause, one record." + coordination-tax lede; one static frame with four lanes (audit retrieval / version control / periodic review / evidence assembly), each carrying its motif from the problem spotlight, its metric (40 min, 3+ live, 1 in 4, 2–3 days), and its tax verbs on a dashed drop; all four converging on one blue "One governed record" bar with per-lane outcomes (found in seconds, one effective version, owner and review date, evidence bound).

## Consensus wins

1. The four-lanes-to-one-bar structure is legible in seconds (clarity 7–8 across all six; the COO and CIO, the hardest graders, gave it 8).
2. The vocabulary is the ICP's own: "effective version" praised by three personas as insider language; the verb pairs (search·verify, compare·confirm, chase the owner, collect·reconcile) repeatedly called "the honest part."
3. Carrying the four problems and metrics over from the spotlight reads as one building argument, not repetition (5/6).

## Consensus failures (ranked)

1. **The magic bar** (6/6): the today-side has mechanism and numbers; the with-Unifize side is a shield icon, a halo, and adjectives. Every persona's next question is some form of "show me the record."
2. **"One governed record" undefined** (5/6): the load-bearing phrase is ambiguous (record vs system of record; per document vs per process) and rhymes with every incumbent's "single source of truth" slide (MasterControl/Veeva named twice).
3. **Coexistence absent** (4/6): "collapses all four into one" pattern-matches to rip-and-replace; the existing stack (eQMS, PLM, M365, ERP) is nowhere in the picture. CIO and Eng Manager gate on this.
4. **Quote-to-stat promotion** (COO, flagged hardest): a scroll ago 40 min and 2–3 days were customer quotes; here they render as universal stats. Confidence upgraded without evidence upgraded.
5. **Persona narrowness** (COO 6, EngMgr 6 on recognition): three of four lanes are quality's documents; no downstream translation (release, holds, delivery) and no engineering artifact (ECO, DHF).
6. **Language flags**: "evidence bound" unclear to 3 personas ("we say evidence attached"); tax/headcount framing reads as strategy-deck language to operators, who live in risk framing ("an out-of-date SOP is an audit finding with my name on it"); a shield gesturing at compliance with no Part 11 / audit-trail words nearby.

## Highest-leverage fixes

1. Cash out the blue bar: replace shield+halo with the anatomy of the governed record (effective version, owner, next review date, e-signature, audit trail, bound evidence) as a slim record strip, ideally the real product; the four checkmarks become labels pointing at visible parts. Asked for, in nearly identical words, by 5 of 6.
2. One compliance line under "governed": Part 11 e-signatures, full audit trail, enforced effective dates.
3. One coexistence cue: the record positioned against email/eQMS/ERP with a write-back caption ("no rip-and-replace").
4. Keep metrics anchored as reported/customer numbers, not universal stats.
5. One downstream line in ops language ("evidence stops blocking release: disposition in hours, not days").
