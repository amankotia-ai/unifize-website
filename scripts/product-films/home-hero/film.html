SOP SOP-118Cleaning validation, coating line 2 Rev E · Effective Controlled copy Tue, Sep 29 · 06:02 KIK. Iyer · Line 2
SOP-118 · REV E · EFFECTIVE SEP 29CONTROLLED COPY

1. Purpose

Defines how coating line 2 is cleaned and checked so every housing meets the 45 to 55 µm coating spec.

2. Scope

Coating heads 1 and 2 on line 2, and the fixtures that hold housings during coating.

3. Materials

IPA, lint-free cloths, fixture gauge FG-12, FRM-201.

4. Procedure

4.1

Stop line 2 and lock out coating head 2 before any cleaning.

4.2

Clean the nozzle and the fixture faces with IPA, then wipe dry with a lint-free cloth.

4.3

Check the head 2 fixture for wear against DWG-2201 Rev D before each shift. Replace the fixture if the locating pins show wear.

New in Rev E
4.4

Record the check and the fixture ID on FRM-201.

4.5

Release the head for production only once the first-piece coating reads 45 to 55 µm.

5. Records

5.1

FRM-201 stays with the batch record for the life of the product.

6. References

DWG-2201 Rev D, FRM-201, NC-204.
