## Champion: `05` The Decision Trace

**The single claim:** *incumbents track document status; Unifize reconstructs the decision trace
across functions.* Every other direction quietly borrowed this as its own strongest line — which is
the tell. For a routing hub a sharp single thesis is the load-bearing asset: it makes the buyer feel
seen at industry altitude (the "two records" contrast *is* their 4pm-on-day-three-of-an-inspection
problem) and gives every downstream module and persona door a reason to exist — each is "where the
claim gets tested." It clears the credibility gate flash-heavy directions can't: it refuses to fake
the trace UI, quarantines the single anonymized `$81,350 / 41%` proof, and uses regulation precisely.

Its weaknesses (RA/COO/CSV demoted to stubs; thin economics) are **additive, graftable fixes — not
architectural flaws.** So the built page is the Decision Trace spine + five grafts.

## Cross-cutting objections the built page MUST resolve

1. **Validated-state silence** — the CSV gatekeeper, in *every* direction, asks: is Unifize itself Part-11-compliant/validated? An auto-reconstructed trace from "ungoverned channels" actively raises a data-integrity flag. → The page needs a first-class "for your validation team" block. *(Built honestly: states only canonical capabilities — Part 11 e-signature, coexistence — and routes deeper validation/security detail to a clearly-forthcoming destination. No invented certifications.)*
2. **Stub-door / vaporware tax** — personas click their fire (CAPA, MDR, their persona page) and hit "coming soon." Only Change Control is live. → Badge live vs. forthcoming honestly; never imply depth that isn't there.
3. **Economic buyer under-served** — translate cost-of-inaction into COO units (cycle-time, missed shipments, trapped working capital) without a fake hero number.
4. **Coexistence stated but not substantiated** — make "sits on your validated QMS — no rip-and-replace" load-bearing and *early*, with named systems of record.
5. **Proof thin / class-mismatched** — keep the single `$81,350/41%` honestly quarantined; keep named logos visually separate from the dollar figure (avoid metric-by-proximity overreach).
6. **Regulatory Affairs demoted** — give RA first-class ingress and a real triage path (the standards filter).
