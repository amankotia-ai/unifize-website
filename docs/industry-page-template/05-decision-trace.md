# 05 · The Decision Trace

> **Direction:** Thesis-first. The page is a single argument.
> **For:** The evaluator running a head-to-head — Unifize vs. MasterControl / Greenlight Guru / ETQ.
> **Must land:** Make the differentiator legible and *defensible*, then route to modules and personas as the surfaces where the claim is proven. **Risk:** abstraction. Antidote: every abstract beat is pinned to a concrete MD artifact (a DHR, a CAPA, a 483, an ECO).
> **Build target:** `/explorations/industry-template` (instanced on Medical Devices). Production `/industries/[slug]` untouched.

---

## 1. Thesis

There is exactly one sentence this page exists to make true in the evaluator's head:

> **Incumbents track document *status*. Unifize reconstructs the decision *trace* across functions.**
> *(canonical — `MD_COMPETITORS.differentiator`, Industries → Medical Devices → Competitive Landscape)*

Every other direction in the bake-off leads with a *symptom* (the Mirror), a *cost* (the Ledger), a *clock* (the War Room), or a *map* (the Atlas). The Decision Trace leads with the **claim itself** and spends the whole page earning the right to have said it. The buyer this is for has already seen three demos. They don't need to be told their world is broken — they need a *category distinction* sharp enough to write into an internal eval doc and defend to a skeptical VP Quality. We hand them that distinction in the first screen, then turn the rest of the page into a chain of evidence for it.

The structural move: **"status" and "trace" become the two poles of the entire page.** Status = what a document-centric QMS records (approved / pending / effective). Trace = the reconstructable reality behind the status — *who decided, on what evidence, against which version, when, and why.* Every section is framed as another place where the gap between those two things shows up: at the audit, in the root cause, in the cost, in each domain, for each persona, in the proof, against each incumbent.

---

## 2. Why *this* shape for a routing hub

A thesis-first page risks becoming a manifesto — beautiful, abstract, and a dead end. This one stays a **routing hub** because the thesis is deliberately constructed to *demand* the two ingress systems as its proof:

- **The claim is unfalsifiable until it's localized.** "Unifize reconstructs the decision trace" is air until you say *which* decisions, in *which* functions. So Section F (Domains × Modules) is reframed from a generic coverage map into **"the nine places a decision trace has to survive"** — CAPA effectiveness, an ECO propagating to the DHR, an MDR reportability call under a 30-day clock. The module map *is* the proof of breadth. The buyer routes into a module page not to "learn more" but to **stress-test the claim** on the workflow they care about.
- **A trace has owners.** A decision isn't traceable in the abstract — *someone* is accountable for being able to reconstruct it under audit. So Section G (Personas) is reframed as **"who gets asked to reconstruct the trace when the investigator is in the room."** The Quality leader owns release confidence and recurrence; the Ops leader owns whether decisions move at all; RA owns the submission and label trail. Routing to a persona page = "show me the trace from where I sit."

So the two non-negotiable ingress blocks aren't bolted on — they're the **two halves of the proof** (breadth = where the trace lives; ownership = who reconstructs it). The thesis can't stand without them, which guarantees they get prime real estate instead of being afterthoughts.

**Altitude discipline holds naturally here.** Because the page is an *argument about a category*, it has every incentive to stay at altitude — it names the decision-trace gap in each domain and persona and then *hands off* to the page that proves it. Rendering the change-control mechanics on this page would actually *weaken* the thesis (it would collapse "we reconstruct the trace everywhere" into "here's one workflow"). The shape polices its own altitude.

### D · Why the Trace Goes Missing — *the structural why*

### E · What a Missing Trace Costs — *the stakes*
The five consequence types as the bill for the gap. **Qualitative only — zero invented dollars on any consequence** (`MD_CONSEQUENCES` is qualitative by design; do-not-publish rule).
- Five named stakes, each one line: **Cycle time** (long cycles; delayed time to market) · **Cost of poor quality** (coordination headcount embedded in COGS) · **Working capital** (trapped cash, quarantine holds) · **Compliance drag** (overdue controls; slow audit & customer proof; lagging post-market signal) · **Revenue risk** (quality escapes & warranty; expanded recall scope; lost market access).
- **The one legitimate number lives here, framed as scale not as a consequence-line:** segment est. annual coordination tax **$75.2M–$808.1M** across the 81 companies (`MD_ECONOMICS.annualTaxLow/High`) — presented as "the size of the gap at the segment level," explicitly *not* attributed to any single consequence row (which keeps the qualitative rule intact).
- **Fear anchor**, stated once, plainly: an **FDA Warning Letter / 483 citing lost traceability between design inputs and outputs**, incomplete CAPA effectiveness evidence, or DHR failure (`primary fear anchor`). This is the thesis's worst-case made concrete.
- **Altitude:** named consequences; no fake metrics, no per-row dollars.

## 5. Fresh visual language — *"the legal-forensic ledger"*

The metaphor governs every choice: this page should feel like a **forensic reconstruction document** — the artifact an investigator or an internal auditor would respect. Authoritative, evidentiary, slightly austere. It deliberately departs from the current site (Geist/JetBrains + blue-violet `oklch(0.528 0.263 262.9)`).

- **Type.** Display in a **high-contrast serif with confident editorial authority** (e.g. *Newsreader* or *Source Serif 4* at 500–600), set tight (`-0.02em`) — serif signals "the record," "the document of record," and instantly reads *not-like-every-other-SaaS-page*. Body in a clean humanist sans (*Inter*) at a **wider measure (66–72ch)** for the argument prose. **Monospace (still JetBrains) is reserved for one job only: trace artifacts** — record IDs (`ECO-2148`, `CAPA-2148`), standards pills, timestamps, signatures. Mono = "this is a record." That single rule makes the status/trace duality *typographic*.
- **Color.** Move off blue-violet to a **forensic ink + amber** scheme:
  - Ground: near-black **ink** `oklch(0.20 0.01 250)` for dark surfaces; warm paper `oklch(0.98 0.004 90)` for light.
  - **Status pole** = neutral graphite (desaturated, inert — `oklch(0.62 0.01 250)`). Status looks *dead*.
  - **Trace pole** = a single warm **amber/signal** accent `oklch(0.74 0.15 70)` used *only* on trace connections, the active decision path, and primary CTAs. Amber = "live, connected, reconstructable." The whole page reads in ink + graphite, and amber appears exactly where the thesis is being proven. Scarce color = high meaning.
  - Use OKLCH throughout for perceptual evenness across the graphite ramp; verify amber-on-ink and ink-on-paper clear WCAG AA (the audit-minded buyer notices contrast failures).
- **Grid & measure.** 12-col, **slightly wider max (~1200px)** per the brief, generous gutters. The argument prose runs in a single wide column (not boxed in cards) so it reads like a *document*, while evidence (the two records, the module map, personas) sits in structured cards. The contrast between flowing argument and structured evidence is the layout's whole personality.
- **The signature device — the trace line.** A thin **amber connector line** that literally draws between the actors/evidence in any "trace" rendering (Section B right card, the hero preview, Section F's value-stream slot). Status cards have *no* connectors — just stacked fields. The presence/absence of the connecting line *is* the differentiator, made graphic. This is the one motif that repeats and ties the page together.
- **Motion (restrained, accessibility-gated).** Reuse the existing `xpl-rise` reveal (14px / 0.6s / reduced-motion respected). One bespoke moment: in Section B, on scroll-into-view, the amber trace line **draws itself** between the trace card's nodes (a 600ms stroke-dashoffset animation) — proving "reconstruction" as a gesture. Nothing else animates. No parallax, no auto-playing anything.
- **Texture.** Almost none — restraint is the point for an audit-credible page. One subtle touch: a faint **hairline ledger rule** (1px, graphite at 8%) under section heads, echoing a ruled form. Dark surfaces get a barely-there grain so the ink doesn't read as flat black.
- **Iconography.** No decorative icons. The only marks are functional: the trace connector, status/trace state dots (graphite dot = recorded; amber dot = reconstructable), and standards pills. Restraint reinforces credibility.
