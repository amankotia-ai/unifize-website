05 Two directions, in context

Same tokens, different convictions about where the brand blue should appear.

Direction A

Editorial

Almost entirely monochrome. Blue is rare and intentional — only on the primary CTA and the active route. Reads like a document.

Direction B

Signal

Blue carries more weight — selected rows, accents on metadata, a tinted surface for the active thread. Still restrained, but more "product".
