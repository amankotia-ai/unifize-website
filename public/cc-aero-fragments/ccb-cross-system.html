What this means
The ECO doesn't depend on email-relay between PLM / ERP / supplier portal / customer portal. Unifize binds the references at the moment of CCB approval — and pushes the resolved payload to each system of record. Email, Teams, supplier emails, design-review notes all feed into the Thread; the Thread is what an FAA or Boeing auditor reads.
