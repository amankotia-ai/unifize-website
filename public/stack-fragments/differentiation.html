01
The approval is a commit point on a Thread — not a checkbox.
Unifize captures why the disposition was approved — the rationale, the evidence supporting it, the participants who agreed. Six months later, when a similar NC appears, that rationale is discoverable. A traditional QMS captures that a CAPA closed; Unifize captures how the decision was reached.
Decision posture
02
Evidence is bound at the time of approval — not assembled at the end.
Test reports, photos, supplier responses, training records, inspection records — all linked to the Thread as created. When an FDA investigator asks for the disposition record and rationale, the response is a click, not a reconstruction exercise.
Evidence binding
03
The approver isn't the coordinator-as-relay.
Routing, ownership, and visibility are enforced by the Process definition. Senior engineers stop being bottlenecks because context-reconstruction time collapses — the Thread already holds it.
Role posture
04
Cross-system / cross-organisation approvals are governed.
The NCR may touch the QMS, ERP (lot/batch), PLM (DHR entry), and a supplier portal. Unifize binds those references into the Thread so the approval doesn't depend on email-relay coordination across system boundaries.
Boundary posture
05
Coexistence — the approval lives where the work lives; the record lives where the QMS lives.
With MasterControl / ETQ / Qualio / Greenlight Guru / Veeva, the approved NC record continues to live in their QMS. Unifize owns the coordination layer that produces the approval; the QMS owns the artifact. Context flows in, approved outcomes flow back.
Coexistence
06
Audit posture is the differentiator.
FDA 820 and ISO 13485 §8.5.2 expect a documented decision trace. If the trace lives in email and meeting notes, the response is days of reconstruction. If it lives in a governed Thread, the response is one record.
Audit posture
