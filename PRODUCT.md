## Product Purpose

Unifize makes the coordination tax visible, measurable and reducible in regulated processes. Cross-functional work (CAPAs, change orders, deviations, supplier decisions, audit evidence) is captured in one accountable thread per event, so decisions, evidence, ownership and completion stay connected and closure is reviewable and auditable as the work happens. Success for the customer is shorter cycle times, cleaner audits and proof generated as a byproduct of execution.

## Positioning

"In regulated processes, cross-functional work runs across siloed teams and fragmented systems. The hidden cost of holding that work together is what we call Coordination Tax. Unifize makes it visible, measurable, and reducible, so that decisions, evidence, and completion stay connected across every team involved." (Positioning Strategy v3.8, resolved 2026-04-10.)

Engagement identity: practitioners who built a platform. Not self-serve SaaS, not a consultancy. Process experience from lived operations, encoded in a configurable platform, with outcomes measured in the customer's environment. Ninety days to prove value on one process, then expand.

## Operating Context

Customers already run systems of record (QMS, ERP, PLM, document repositories, often MES and LIMS). The coordination that produces their records runs through email, meetings and spreadsheets. Unifize coexists with those systems; it does not replace them. Standards in play include 21 CFR Part 11, ISO 13485, ISO 9001, IATF 16949, AS9100, cGMP, FSMA, 21 CFR Part 111. Products the market recognises: QMS, DMS, PLM, MES.

## Product Principles

1. The waste is between teams, not inside them; name it and measure it.
2. Systems of record stay authoritative; Unifize closes the gap between them.
3. Proof is a byproduct of the work, complete at sign-off.
4. People own accountability; process governs structure; AI accelerates; outcomes are measured, not claimed.
5. Practitioners who built a platform: work alongside the customer, prove value in ninety days, then expand.
